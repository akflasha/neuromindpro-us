  • Vitamin B1 (Thiamine HCL): Supports normal nervous system function and helps the body convert nutrients into energy used for everyday mental and physical activity.
  • Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxine HCL): Supports normal nervous system function and plays a nutritional role in processes involved in brain function and neurotransmitter activity.

  • Caffeine from Green Coffee Bean: Included to support mental alertness, attention, and everyday focus.

  • Huperzia Serrata Whole Herb Extract: Included to complement support for memory, recall, concentration, and overall cognitive function.
